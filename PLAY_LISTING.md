## Full Description (max 4000 characters)
Discover peace through pure logic with ZenHitori, a calm and meditative Japanese number puzzle based on the celebrated Nikoli pencil-and-paper classic "Hitori" (Hitori ni shite kure — "Leave me alone!").

In ZenHitori, your objective is to bring balance to a grid of numbers by shading away conflicting duplicates using polished black river stones, while preserving the harmony of the remaining unshaded stream.

### 🪨 The Three Noble Rules
• **No Duplicates**: In every row and column, no number may appear more than once among the unshaded (white) cells.
• **No Touching Black Stones**: Shaded (black) cells cannot touch horizontally or vertically. They may only touch diagonally.
• **One Continuous Stream**: All unshaded numbers must form a single, unbroken connected component across the board. No isolated numbers or surrounded corners!

### ✨ Zen Features
• **50 Handcrafted Levels**: 5 difficulty chapters ranging from gentle 5×5 introductory gardens to intricate 9×9 stone sanctuaries:
  - Spring Garden (5×5)
  - Koi River (6×6)
  - Bamboo Forest (7×7)
  - Stone Path (8×8)
  - Zen Sanctuary (9×9)
• **Pure Procedural Acoustics**: Real-time synthesized acoustics via Web Audio API—smooth river stone placement clicks, Tibetan singing bowl ring harmonics, crisp bamboo clacks, and resonant zen temple gongs upon puzzle completion. No internet downloads or audio files required.
• **Smart Deduction Hints**: Get gentle guidance whenever you need insight, with clear deductive explanations based on classic Japanese solving techniques.
• **Fluid Touch Controls**: Flexible input modes including Cycle mode, Direct Stone shading, Direct White circling, and smooth drag-painting across cells. Full unlimited undo and reset support.
• **100% Offline & Private**: Zero data collection, no internet connection required, no timer pressure, and no advertisements. Play peacefully anytime, anywhere.
