How to Play Hitori

1
No Duplicate Numbers: In each row and column, no number can appear more than once among the unshaded (white) cells.
2
No Touching Black Stones: Shaded (black) cells cannot touch each other horizontally or vertically (diagonal touching is allowed).
3
Connected Stream: All unshaded (white) numbers must remain connected as a single continuous group. No isolated numbers or cutoffs!
💡
Pro Tip: When you shade a cell black, all 4 of its neighbors are immediately safe (white). When a cell is confirmed white, all identical numbers in its row and column must be shaded!
